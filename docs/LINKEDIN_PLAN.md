## Launch post draft

I have completed the first version of ATLAS Logistics Analytics, a practical project developed to connect logistics operations with data and systems.

The project follows a simple discipline: problem, data, method, validation, insight and decision.

Across eight stages, I worked on Excel validation, Power Query and data quality, dimensional modelling, Power BI and DAX, SQL, logistics KPIs, executive storytelling and systems integration.

The most important lesson was not how quickly a tool or AI can produce an answer. It was learning to test whether that answer is logically, technically and operationally correct.
