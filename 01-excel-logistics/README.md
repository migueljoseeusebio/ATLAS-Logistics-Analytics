# 01 — Excel Logistics Validation

## Problem

Operational shipment data needs to be reconciled and analysed without allowing formula errors or inconsistent references to distort the result.

## Method

The exercise applies lookup, conditional aggregation, date logic, filters, structured tables and pivot tables. Calculations are checked against known totals and exception cases.

## Validation

- Confirm source and output row counts.
- Test lookup keys for missing or duplicate values.
- Recalculate selected records manually.
- Compare pivot totals with direct formulas.
- Check blank and error handling explicitly.

## Decision supported

Establish whether the operational workbook is reliable enough to support service and cost analysis.

## Public evidence

The workbook will be added after document-property, hidden-content and formula review. A screenshot should show the result without exposing unnecessary raw data.
